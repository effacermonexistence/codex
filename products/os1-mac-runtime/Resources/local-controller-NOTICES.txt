OS-1 local controller — verified first-run acquisition

The OS-1 package includes a pinned acquisition contract and installer. It does
not contain the OpenClaw npm dependency tree, Node.js runtime, Ollama binary,
or Qwen model weights. Those public artifacts are fetched and verified during
first-run provisioning. Until that succeeds, local-controller readiness is
blocked; the app must not call this an offline or fully bundled installation.
